# Elevator Pitch

> [!quote] 10 seconds
> **TimeLogic is attendance you can actually trust.** Employees clock in from their phone, and the system proves they're really there — on the company Wi-Fi, on their own registered device, at the right time.

> [!quote] 30 seconds
> Most attendance apps are easy to cheat: people clock in from home, share phones, or send a friend to punch them in ("buddy punching"). TimeLogic stops all of that with **three independent checks on every single check-in** — network, device, and a time-based code. Admins run everything from a desktop app; employees use a phone app; everything syncs live through a secure cloud backend. It already runs on **Android, Desktop, and iPhone/web**, and scales to **thousands of employees across hundreds of organizations**.

---

## The hook (use one)
- "Clocking in from bed is over."
- "Three checks. All must pass. Or it doesn't count."
- "Buddy punching, solved."

## Who it's for
Any organization that needs **honest, location-verified attendance** — offices, factories, clinics, schools, agencies, multi-branch businesses.

## Why people care
- **Owners/HR** — accurate hours, no payroll fraud, exportable records.
- **Admins** — one dashboard for sessions, leave, breaks, fraud alerts.
- **Employees** — a clean, fast phone app; check in, take breaks, request leave.
