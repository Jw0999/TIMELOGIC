# Slide Deck Outline

> [!tip] Target: 12 slides, ~10–12 minutes + demo. Each slide links to its source note.

| # | Slide | Say this | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Title** | "TimeLogic — attendance you can actually trust." Logo + your name. | [[Elevator Pitch]] |
| 2 | **The problem** | Buddy punching, remote clock-in, phone sharing, lost data → inflated payroll. | [[Problem & Solution]] |
| 3 | **The idea** | 3 independent checks on every check-in. All must pass. | [[The 3-Layer Verification]] |
| 4 | **Layer 1 · Network** | On the company Wi-Fi/IP, or it doesn't count. (Auto-learned IP for iPhone.) | [[The 3-Layer Verification]] |
| 5 | **Layer 2 · Device** | One phone per employee; borrowed phones rejected; admin can reset. | [[The 3-Layer Verification]] |
| 6 | **Layer 3 · Time** | One-time, short-lived code; replays & scripts dead. | [[The 3-Layer Verification]] |
| 7 | **More than check-in** | Sessions auto-run, breaks, leave, fraud alerts, exports, never delete. | [[Features]] |
| 8 | **Who uses it** | Super-Admin / Admin / Employee — purpose-built apps. | [[User Roles]] |
| 9 | **Platforms** | Android, iPhone/web PWA, Desktop, Super-Admin, Marketing — all live. | [[Platforms & Download Links]] |
| 10 | **Architecture** | Layered backend, multi-tenant, real-time. (Show diagram.) | [[Architecture]] |
| 11 | **Why it's clean** | Thin controllers/fat services, one source of truth, DRY, no dead code. | [[How It's Clean]] |
| 12 | **Live demo + close** | Run [[Demo Script]]; end on the numbers. | [[Demo Script]] |

## Closing numbers slide (memorize)
**3** layers · **5** live surfaces · **1,000+** orgs · **0** records lost · **100%** configurable · **24/7** backend.

## Story arc
**Pain → Idea → Proof (3 layers) → Breadth (features/platforms) → Credibility (architecture/clean) → Show it (demo) → Numbers.**
