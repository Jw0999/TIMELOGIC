# Target Market Overview

## 📈 The market
- **MEA time-&-attendance software:** ~**$276M (2025) → $509.4M (2034), ~7% CAGR** — driven by government modernization, HR digitization, cloud adoption.
- **75% of companies** lose money to **buddy punching**; worst in manual-timekeeping sectors (construction, manufacturing, retail).
- **Gartner:** ~**70% of large employers** will track staff digitally by end-2025; **44%** of employees admit to "coffee-badging."

## 🇳🇬 The Nigerian wedge: ghost workers + proxy attendance
- **Katsina:** 3,488 ghost workers removed → **₦5.7B** projected savings.
- **45,000** ghost workers exposed in federal payroll (via BVN).
- **Kano** (44 LGAs) + **Rivers** rolling out biometric payroll verification.
- **Insight:** audits are *one-off*. TimeLogic is the **daily** verification layer.

## 🎯 Segment priority
1. **Tier 1 — distributed, hours-billed:** security firms, HR outsourcing, facility management. Best ROI, fastest close.
2. **Tier 2 — high seat count:** manufacturing, QSR/retail chains, hospitals, hotels.
3. **Tier 3 — bigger/slower:** schools, government/LGAs.
