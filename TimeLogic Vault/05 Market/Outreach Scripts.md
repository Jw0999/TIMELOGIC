# Outreach Scripts

> [!tip] How to use
> Swap `{{Name}}`, `{{Company}}`, `{{your name}}`, `{{phone}}`. Keep emails under 120 words. Always end with the live links. Demo link: **https://timelogic-app.pages.dev** · Site: **https://timelogic-web.pages.dev**. See [[Named Prospects]] for who to send to.

Common proof points to weave in:
- **No hardware** — runs on staff's own phones + your office Wi-Fi/IP.
- **3 checks per check-in** — network · device · time. → [[The 3-Layer Verification]]
- **Beats buddy-punching & ghost workers** — daily, not a yearly audit.
- **Nothing deleted** — full audit trail, Excel/CSV export.
